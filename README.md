## 🛠 Troubleshooting common issues

If the connection fails, try these methods to fix the problem:

- Check your phone connection: WhatsApp needs a stable Wi-Fi or mobile data signal to keep the bridge open.
- Restart the application: Close the Baileys window completely and open it again.
- Clear the cache: Sometimes old data blocks the connection. Restarting the app often clears these temporary errors.
- Firewall settings: If your computer blocks the connection, allow the application through your Windows firewall settings.
- Phone battery: If your phone runs out of battery, the connection drops. Keep your phone charged for continuous service.

## 📋 Frequently asked questions

Do I need to program anything?
No. Baileys handles the code in the background. You only need to scan the QR code to use it.

Is the software safe?
The app runs locally on your computer. It encrypts your communication to protect your privacy while you use the interface.

Does it stay connected forever?
The connection stays active as long as your phone has internet access. If you turn off your phone or lose signal, you will need to let the app resync once your phone is back online.
